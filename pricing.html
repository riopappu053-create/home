Frequently Addressed

Common Questions Regarding Pricing.

Straightforward answers about our quoting policies, hidden fee guarantees, and warranty terms.

Are there any hidden plumbing or disposal fees?

No. Our in-home estimate covers all copper press materials, brass isolation valves, drain line hookups, old tank haul away, and tax.

What if my home does not have a pre-plumbed loop?

If your home lacks a dedicated loop, our technicians will tie directly into your primary main shut-off. Any minor line work is quoted up front before we start.

How much does salt cost to operate annually?

Because our valves use meter-proportional regeneration, an average family of four uses only 6 to 9 bags per year, equating to under $75 in annual salt.

Does the equipment warranty transfer if I sell my home?

Yes. Our 10-year comprehensive warranty transfers automatically to subsequent property owners, enhancing appraisal and buyer confidence.
