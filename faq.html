Frequently Asked Questions

Everything About Installation, Salts & Health.

Filter answers or search directly by technical topic.

How does an ion-exchange softener eliminate hard water minerals without chemical taste?
Ion-exchange technology uses high-grade spherical resin beads charged with mild sodium or potassium ions. As raw water passes through the pressure vessel, mineral ions like calcium ($Ca^{2+}$) and magnesium ($Mg^{2+}$) bond magnetically to the resin and exchange for sodium. Because only trace levels of minerals are swapped, the water carries no salty flavor while completely preventing scale buildup.
Will soft water make my skin and hair feel slippery after showering?
That silky, smooth sensation is your natural skin lipids completely free from chalky mineral soap curd. In hard water, calcium reacts with soaps to leave an insoluble curd film that clogs pores and dries skin. Soft water rinses soap clean, restoring natural hydration and reducing eczema irritation.
How long does installation take, and will my water be shut off all day?
A standard residential installation takes between 3 to 5 hours. Your main water line is shut off for only 45 to 60 minutes while our master plumbers sweat the primary copper tie-ins and mount the 3-valve bypass manifold. Once bypassed, full household pressure is restored while we calibrate tanks and brine electronics.
What if my home doesn't have a pre-plumbed water softener loop?
Many homes lack a pre-built loop. Our licensed plumbers tap into your main water line where it enters the property. We route clean copper or expansion PEX-A lines to your utility area, isolate outdoor hose bibbs to prevent wasting softened water on lawns, and tie drain lines into code-approved air gaps.
Does a water softener eliminate toxic contaminants like PFAS and microplastics?
No. Softeners are engineered exclusively to exchange mineral hardness ions (calcium and magnesium). To strip PFAS "forever chemicals", pharmaceutical residues, and chlorine, we integrate an upstream catalytic activated carbon bed, paired with an under-sink reverse osmosis system for dedicated drinking and cooking water.
What is covered under the 10-Year Comprehensive System Warranty?
Our warranty covers the fiberglass composite pressure vessel, digital metering valve body, internal drive motor, optical sensors, and 10% cross-linked resin matrix against defects or breakdown. Both genuine replacement parts and certified technician labor are included with zero deductible.
