01

True Ion-Exchange Softening

Our NSF-certified resin beads trap harsh calcium and magnesium ions, replacing them with benign sodium or potassium ions for complete mineral relief.

On-Site Chemistry Analysis

We Don't Guess Your Water Chemistry. We Test It To The Grain.

Every municipal reservoir and private water well carries distinct chemical profiles. Our certified plumbing technicians run a certified digital test of your tap water before engineering a solution.

Hardness (GPG)

Measures exact grains per gallon to set valve regeneration frequency precisely.

TDS Count (PPM)

Evaluates aggregate dissolved solids, salts, and dissolved metals.

Chlorine / Chloramine

Detects caustic municipal disinfectants that deteriorate rubber plumbing seals.

Iron & pH Levels

Identifies acidic corrosion potential to prevent pinhole piping leaks.
